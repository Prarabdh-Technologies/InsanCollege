Welcome to Insan Degree College

A community-centered approach to education since 1966.

At Insan Degree College, education is a shared responsibility. Our community-centered philosophy connects learning with self-development, self-help, and self-reliance.

Here in Shikshanagar, Kishanganj, we aim to help students grow into independent individuals and responsible citizens, with educators supporting them along the way.

Our philosophy of work

“In a way, education means independence.”

Our institution is community centered. We believe in total development. We feel that education should lead to self-development. This development should take place by means of self-help and self-reliance.

In a way, education means independence. It should make us free from others. We should become capable of running our own affairs without the help of others as far as it is possible.

Hence, we the educators are here to facilitate and to assist this process of development.

Dr. Syed HasanFounder
